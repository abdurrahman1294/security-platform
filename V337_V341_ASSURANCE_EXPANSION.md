# V3.37–V3.41 Assurance Expansion

The platform now has a common specialist contract and deeper safe assurance layers before the next full test campaign.

## Canonical lifecycle

`preflight → approval → execution → evidence → failure → finding → validation → remediation → retest`

The lifecycle is a data contract. It does not grant authority to a specialist. Every consequential operation remains subject to scope, authorization and the relevant approval mechanism.

## Specialist coverage

V3.37 normalizes existing capability metadata into a common adapter registry and exposes missing specialist wiring instead of pretending that a catalog entry is executable.

## Web/API depth

V3.38 expands the assessment corpus to twenty bounded classes. These are assurance/test definitions and observation plans, not an unrestricted exploit generator.

## Fuzzing

V3.39 adds campaign lifecycle metadata: seed policy, case budgets, corpus persistence, deduplication, minimization and crash triage. Actual fuzzing remains a specialist/testbed responsibility.

## Engagement analytics

V3.40 creates a local engagement index for long-running assessment analytics without replacing the evidence artifacts or inventing results.

## Identity

V3.41 adds a broad protocol/check matrix while keeping credentials operator-supplied and excluding credential theft, spraying and bypass execution.

## Explicit remaining boundary

A general-purpose autonomous endpoint agent, unrestricted exploit runtime, credential theft/capture, covert C2, persistence deployment, destructive actions and uncontrolled propagation remain intentionally outside autonomous execution.
