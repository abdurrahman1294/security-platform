# V3.45–V3.47 Validation Expansion

V3.45 adds an isolated multi-domain integration range with disposable loopback web and static/config fixtures: 30 seeded scenarios across web/API, network, identity, cloud, endpoint, mobile, firmware/IoT, OT/ICS, automotive, containers, source/supply-chain, and fuzzing.

V3.46 adds campaign-quality scoring for detection, validation, evidence, and governance. Scores explicitly describe fixture effectiveness rather than real-world detection accuracy.

V3.47 adds deterministic failure-injection/resilience coverage and extends the one-command campaign to run the integration range, quality scoring, and resilience checks.

Safety boundary: all fixtures are local/disposable; no external targeting, real secrets, credential theft, persistence, covert C2, destructive impact, propagation, or unrestricted RCE is implemented.
