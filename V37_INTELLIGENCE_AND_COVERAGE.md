# V3.7 Intelligence & Coverage Pass

V3.7 deepens the assessment lifecycle with evidence-only intelligence rather than unrestricted offensive automation.

## Added
- Asset identity resolution and alias normalization
- Explicit dependency mapping
- Authenticated-result normalization
- Evidence-backed attack-path ranking
- Coverage-gap detection
- Remediation prioritization
- Engagement state machine with validated transitions
- Engagement-runner integration
- `securityctl intelligence`

## Safety boundary
These features reason over supplied evidence and do not expand authorization, discover new scope, execute arbitrary commands, provide exploit instructions, or claim reachability from correlation alone.
