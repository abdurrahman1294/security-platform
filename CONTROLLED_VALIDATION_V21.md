# V2.1 Controlled Validation & Attack-Chain Expansion

V2.1 expands the disposable local lab with evidence-driven validation for XSS reflection, boolean SQL injection, IDOR, path traversal, open redirect, exposed configuration, and weak session-cookie flags. It also validates four benign persistence simulations and three bounded second-host authentication methods.

All active validation is loopback-only and non-destructive. R4 actions remain governed by ROE and one-time approval. No arbitrary shell, stealth, C2, credential spraying, uncontrolled propagation, or real-data exfiltration is provided.

The evidence reasoner is deterministic and transparent: confirmed findings produce justified next-action recommendations instead of blind chaining.
